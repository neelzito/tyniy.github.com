Features

Simple outside. Careful inside.

Library scan

See where the space is

Tyniy scans your library and shows how much each kind of photo and video could free, then compresses them with one tap.

Engine

jpegli encoder

Photos are encoded with jpegli, a modern JPEG encoder that targets what the human eye can actually see.

Videos

Smaller videos

An HEVC encoder tries several quality steps for each clip and keeps the smallest result that still looks the same.

Live Photos

Still Live Photos

The photo and its motion are compressed together and checked as a pair before anything is replaced.

Safety

Originals you can restore

Replaced originals go to Recently Deleted in Photos or the Trash on the Mac. On an SD card, Tyniy asks before overwriting.

Metadata

EXIF and colors kept

Camera, lens, date, GPS, orientation and color profiles carry over, so Display P3 and HDR photos keep their colors.

Formats

JPEG, HEIC, PNG, MP4, MOV

Screenshots shrink a lot while staying sharp. Optionally convert photos to WebP or AVIF for websites.

Mac

Watch folders, Finder, Shortcuts

Compress new files in folders like Downloads automatically, right-click in Finder, or use the "Compress Files" action.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your device

No accounts, no uploads, no analytics. Tyniy works completely offline.

For photographers

Compress on the card, before you import.

Connect your camera's SD card to your iPhone or iPad with a card reader. Tyniy compresses the photos straight from the card, so only the smaller files end up in your library, your laptop or your cloud.

1

Plug in the card

Use any USB-C or Lightning SD card reader. The card shows up in the Files app, with no import needed.

2

Choose from Files

In Tyniy, tap Choose from Files and pick photos or the whole DCIM folder. Tyniy compresses them right away.

3

Replace on the card

Tap Save to Files, then Replace Originals. The smaller files take the originals' place on the card, ready to import.

A card has no Recently Deleted, so Tyniy asks before overwriting anything. Prefer to keep the originals? Save the smaller files to another folder instead. Works with JPEG, HEIC, PNG, MP4 and MOV, and with external SSDs and USB drives too.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I compress photos on my iPhone without losing quality?

Install Tyniy, tap + and pick photos and videos, or scan your library to see what can get smaller. Tyniy compresses each photo only as far as it can without a visible change, then puts the smaller version in your library with its date, location, favorites and albums. iOS asks you to confirm before anything is replaced.

Can I get my original photos back?

Yes. Replaced originals go to Recently Deleted in Photos on iPhone and iPad, and to the Trash on the Mac, where you can restore them until they're emptied. Files on an SD card or a drive connected to an iPhone or iPad are overwritten in place, so Tyniy asks first, and you can save to another folder instead.

Why does Tyniy say some photos are “already small”?

Tyniy leaves a file untouched when it can't be made meaningfully smaller. Photos taken with a recent iPhone in HEIC are often already efficient. Older JPEGs, photos from cameras, screenshots and videos usually shrink the most.

Does Tyniy compress videos and Live Photos?

Yes. Videos are compressed with HEVC, trying several quality steps per clip and keeping the smallest result that still looks the same. Live Photos are compressed together with their motion and stay Live Photos.

Is Tyniy really free?

Yes. Tyniy is free on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no limits.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Tyniy compresses everything on your device and makes no network connections. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Is Tyniy a free JPEGmini alternative?

Yes. Like JPEGmini, Tyniy shrinks photos without visible loss, but it's free without daily limits, runs on iPhone and iPad as well as the Mac, and includes PNG, video, WebP and AVIF output and watch folders. See the comparison.
