The app

Tyniy compresses photos (JPEG, HEIC and PNG) and videos (MP4 and MOV) on your device. It can replace originals, save compressed copies, convert photos to WebP or AVIF, resize large photos and watch folders on the Mac. Tyniy is free, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads.

Replacing originals

When you choose to replace originals, Tyniy moves them to the Trash on the Mac or to Recently Deleted in Photos on iPhone and iPad. They can be restored from there until the Trash is emptied or Photos removes them, usually after 30 days.

On iPhone and iPad, "Replace Originals" for files from Files (for example on an SD card or an external drive) overwrites the originals where they are. There is no Recently Deleted for these files, so they can't be restored. Tyniy asks you to confirm first.

Either way, keep backups of photos and videos that matter to you.
